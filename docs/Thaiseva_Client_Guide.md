# Thaiseva Food Portal - Non-Technical Platform Guide

This document explains how the Thaiseva Food Portal operates in simple, non-technical terms. It covers how partners join, how menus are managed, and how food orders flow from the customer to the delivery rider.

---

## 1. How Everything Works (Overview)

The Thaiseva Food Portal is a centralized platform connecting **Master Admins** (you, the platform owners), **Partner Restaurants** (hotels and kitchens), **Customers**, and **Delivery Riders**.

- **Master Admins** oversee the entire network. They approve new restaurants, manage the master list of all food items, and finalize deliveries.
- **Partner Restaurants** manage their own kitchens. They see incoming orders, prepare the food, and mark it ready for pickup. They also toggle which dishes from the master list are currently available in their kitchen.
- **Customers** use the customer app to browse menus and place food orders.
- **Riders** pick up the food from the restaurant and deliver it to the customer.

Everything happens in **real-time**. When a customer places an order, it instantly appears on the restaurant's kitchen screen without them needing to refresh the page.

## 2. Partner Onboarding: How Signups Work

When a new restaurant or hotel wants to join Thaiseva, they follow a simple onboarding process:

1. **Application Submission:** The restaurant owner goes to the public signup page on the platform. They fill out their business details and upload their required documents (like a Business License and ID card).
2. **Pending Review:** Once submitted, the application enters a "Pending" state. The restaurant owner cannot log in or accept orders yet.
3. **Master Admin Verification:** As the Master Admin, you will see this pending application in your dashboard. You review their details and documents. 
4. **Approval & Commission Setting:** If everything looks good, you approve the application. At this time, you also set the **Commission Percentage** that the platform will take from this restaurant's future orders.
5. **Account Creation:** Upon your approval, the system automatically creates an official account for the restaurant owner. They can now log in to their Partner Dashboard and start receiving orders.

## 3. How Menus & Hotels Are Accepted

Thaiseva uses a **Decentralized Menu** system, meaning each restaurant has full control over their own unique menu offerings.

Here is how menus work:

1. **Restaurant Control:** Once approved, the restaurant owner logs into their Partner Dashboard to build their menu. They can independently add new dishes, set prices, write descriptions, and upload pictures.
2. **Availability:** Restaurants can easily toggle individual dishes "On" or "Off" depending on if their kitchen has the ingredients today.
3. **Master Admin Oversight:** While partners create and manage their own items, the Master Admin still has oversight and can intervene or review menus if necessary.
4. **Customer View:** Customers view a unique menu for each specific restaurant. They only see dishes that the restaurant has currently toggled "On."

## 4. How Order Status Changes (From Start to Finish)

When a customer orders food, the order goes through a strict step-by-step process. Here is exactly how an order moves:

1. **New Order (Customer places the order)**
   - The customer submits the order.
   - The restaurant hears a notification sound and sees the "New Order" appear on their screen.

2. **Order Confirm (Master Admin reviews)**
   - The Master Admin reviews the incoming order and clicks "Accept" to verify it. (They can also "Reject" it if necessary, which cancels the order).
   - The customer is notified that their order has been accepted.

3. **Preparing (Cooking begins)**
   - The restaurant clicks "Start Preparing" when the chef begins cooking. 

4. **Ready for Pickup (Food is bagged)**
   - When the food is cooked and bagged, the restaurant clicks "Ready."
   - The system signals that the order is waiting for delivery.
   - **Rider Assignment:** The Master Admin selects an available Rider and assigns them to this specific order.

5. **Out for Delivery (Rider takes over)**
   - Once a Rider is assigned and arrives at the kitchen, the restaurant (or Master Admin) hands over the food and changes the status to "Out for Delivery."
   - The Rider is now on their way to the customer's location with the food.

6. **Delivered (Order completed)**
   - Once the food physically reaches the customer, the Master Admin confirms the delivery in the system.
   - The status becomes "Delivered."
   - **Financial Split:** At this exact moment, the system automatically splits the money. It calculates the Platform's Commission and the Restaurant's Revenue Share, adding it to their respective earning dashboards.

**Cancellations:** If an order is cancelled at any step before delivery, it becomes "Cancelled" and the process stops.

---

## 5. Summary

In short, **Master Admins** act as the gatekeepers and managers. You approve the restaurants, create the standard menus, and finalize the deliveries to distribute the money. **Partner Restaurants** focus purely on what they do best: accepting orders, cooking food, and handing it off to the riders. Everything is connected seamlessly in real-time.
