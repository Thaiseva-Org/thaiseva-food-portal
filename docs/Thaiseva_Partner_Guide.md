# Thaiseva Food Portal - Partner Operations Guide

Welcome to the Thaiseva Food Portal! This guide is designed specifically for **Partner Restaurants and Hotels**. It explains everything you need to know from the moment you sign up, to managing your menu, handling daily orders, and tracking your earnings.

---

## 1. Getting Started: The Signup Process

Joining the Thaiseva platform is simple. Here is how your onboarding works:

1. **Submit Application:** Go to the public signup portal and fill out the onboarding application. Select every cuisine your restaurant serves from the cuisine dropdown, provide your business and contact details, and upload the required legal documents (such as your Business License or ID).
2. **Pending Approval:** Once submitted, your application enters a "Pending" state. During this time, the Master Admin team is reviewing your details to ensure everything is correct.
3. **Approval & Account Creation:** Once the Master Admin verifies your application, your account is officially activated. The platform automatically sets your agreed-upon commission rate, and you can now log in to the Partner Dashboard using your credentials.

---

## 2. Managing Your Menu (Decentralized Catalog)

Thaiseva gives you complete control over your own menu. You are not forced to pick from a master list; you create and manage your dishes independently.

### Adding New Dishes
- Navigate to the **Food Items** section of your dashboard.
- You can create a new dish by entering the title, description, category (e.g., Appetizer, Main Course), selecting one or more cuisines, uploading a high-quality photo, and setting your base price.
- If you want to offer a promotion, you can apply a discount percentage directly to the item.

### Toggling Availability
- **Sold Out for the Day?** You don't need to delete an item if you run out of ingredients. Simply toggle the dish's status to **"Off"** or "Hidden." It will instantly disappear from the customer app.
- Once you have the ingredients back in stock, toggle it **"On"** and customers can order it again immediately.

### Restaurant Profile and Account
- Update the restaurant name and owner name from **Restaurant Profile → Edit restaurant info**.
- Your account email is read-only. Contact the Master Admin if the restaurant sign-in email must be changed.
- The Account Settings section is used for changing your current password.

## 3. The Kitchen Order Board (Handling Delivery Orders)

When a customer places an order, your dashboard updates in **real-time** (with a notification sound). You must process the order through a strict set of stages:

1. **New Order (Pending Master Admin):** A new ticket appears on your board. The Master Admin will first review and "Accept" this order on behalf of the platform.
2. **Order Confirmed:** Once accepted by the Master Admin, the order is officially confirmed.
3. **Start Preparing:** When your chef begins cooking, click "Start Preparing." This updates the customer that their food is on the stove.
4. **Ready for Pickup:** As soon as the food is cooked and packed in the delivery bag, click "Ready." This signals to the Master Admin and the assigned Delivery Rider that they can take the food.
5. **Handing it Off:** Once the Rider arrives, hand over the food and transition the status to **"Out for Delivery."** Your job for this order is now complete! The Rider and Master Admin will handle the final delivery confirmation.

---

## 4. Earnings & Commission Splits

You don't need to manually calculate how much you owe the platform. The system does this automatically on every successful order.

- **The Split:** Only when an order is successfully marked as **"Delivered"** by the Master Admin/Rider, the system calculates the split.
- **Revenue Share:** The platform automatically subtracts the agreed-upon Commission Percentage from the food subtotal. The remaining amount is credited to your account as your "Revenue Share."
- **Dashboard Tracking:** You can view your daily, weekly, and monthly earnings directly in your dashboard's stats section, giving you full transparency into your business performance.
